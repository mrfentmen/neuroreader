## WHAT NEUROREADER IS

NeuroReader is a free reading tool for neurodivergent people (ADHD, dyslexia, autism). It transforms text using a Variable Fixation Formula that bolds parts of words and all punctuation to help neurodivergent brains read without losing focus, skipping lines, or drowning in visual noise.

The key innovation: unlike Bionic Reading (which uses a fixed formula), NeuroReader varies the bolding within defined ranges. This variable stimulation prevents the brain from habituating to a fixed pattern — a core problem for ADHD readers.

---

## THE FOUNDER

The founder is neurodivergent themselves. They built NeuroReader because existing tools (OpenDyslexic, Bionic Reading) either do not work well, are ugly, or charge money for basic reading help. The founder believes reading is not a luxury and that neurodivergent people deserve tools built specifically for their brains.

## MONETIZATION MODEL

NeuroReader has no premium tier. All features are free forever. Revenue comes from:

1. **Display ads** (banner only, never over text) on web and Android
2. **Government grants** (accessibility, neurodivergence, disability tech)
3. **Font licensing** (free personal use, paid business license)
4. **Optional donations** (GitHub Sponsors / Buy Me a Coffee)

This model exists because the vows forbid paywalling reading help.

---

## COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

- **Bionic Reading (bionic-reading.com):** Patented fixed formula, charges money, one-size-fits-all. They own patents on deterministic word-part bolding. NeuroReader's variable formula is legally distinct.
- **OpenDyslexic:** Free but widely considered ugly. Static font swap that does not change how reading works.
- **Microsoft Immersive Reader:** Free but buried in Edge browser. Not a destination product.
- **BeeLine Reader:** Color gradients, considered gimmicky. Not neurodivergent-specific.

Nobody owns "reading tool specifically for neurodivergent brains with variable fixation." That is the gap.

## RISKS

- **Legal:** Bionic Reading could challenge despite documented differentiation. Mitigation: open-source the formula to prove independent creation; consult lawyer if contacted.
- **Ad revenue:** May be low initially. Mitigation: pursue government grants early; keep costs at $0.
- **Adoption:** Hard to get first users. Mitigation: post in neurodivergent communities (Reddit, TikTok, Twitter) where word-of-mouth is strong.
- **Technical:** Variable formula must work correctly across edge cases. Mitigation: thorough testing, iterate with real users.
