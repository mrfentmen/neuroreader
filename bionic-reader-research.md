# Bionic Reader — read like your brain works

> A reading tool built specifically for neurodivergent brains, by someone who has one.

---

## The problem
Neurodivergent people (ADHD, dyslexia, autism, etc.) struggle with reading — but not all in the same way. ADHD brains lose focus and wander. Dyslexic brains scramble letters and tire fast. Autistic brains get overwhelmed by clutter and unpredictability. Every "reading aid" out there is one-size-fits-all, and it doesn't fit.

Existing products:
- **Bionic Reading** (bionic-reading.com) — bolds first letters of words, generic speed reading. Not neurodivergent-specific.
- **Speechify / NaturalReader** — text-to-speech, one feature
- **BeeLine Reader** — color gradients, gimmicky
- **Microsoft Immersive Reader** — some good features but buried in Edge, not a destination
- **Readwise Reader** — organization tool, not a reading aid

None of them are built FROM the neurodivergent experience. None adapt to YOUR specific brain.

---

## What it is
A reading tool that adapts to how YOUR brain reads, not how "normal" brains read.

**Core features:**

1. **Neurotype modes** — pick your brain, get your setup:
   - **ADHD mode**: Focus timer + chunking + gamified streaks. Text breaks into small sections. Dopamine-friendly progress (streaks, visual rewards, "you read for 12 min!"). Body-double option (read virtually alongside others).
   - **Dyslexia mode**: OpenDyslexic/specialized fonts, generous spacing, ruler/tracking line, optional color overlays. Syllable breaks. No justified text (rivers of white space).
   - **Autism mode**: Ultra-clean layout, predictable structure, reduced clutter, consistent formatting, no animations/popups, optional monotony (same font/spacing always).
   - **Custom mode**: Mix and match. Because neurodivergence doesn't come in neat boxes.

2. **Import anything** — paste any URL, PDF, article, book. Bionic Reader strips it down and re-formats it for your brain.

3. **Reading sprints** — timed bursts (5, 10, 15 min) with optional accountability. Works with ADHD dopamine, not against it.

4. **Reading together (body doubling)** — see others reading "with" you. Silent co-reading. Huge for ADHD accountability. Optional camaraderie without pressure.

5. **Progress that feels good** — not shaming "you're behind." Visual streaks, time-read tracking, "you've read 47 hours this month." Celebrate the win.

6. **Reading queue** — save articles, they auto-format for your brain. No more "I'll read this later" tabs that rot.

---

## Why it's novel
- **Not generic.** Every existing reading aid assumes a neurotypical brain with one quirk. Bionic_reader assumes neurodivergence is the design center.
- **Built by neurodivergent, for neurodivergent.** The lived experience shows in the details — no shaming, no "just try harder," no overwhelming UI.
- **Community.** Reading together, sharing what you're reading, accountability groups. Reading is lonely; this makes it less so.
- **Adapts to you, not the other way around.** You don't learn to use Bionic Reader. It learns how you read.

## Name situation
"Bionic Reading" (bionic-reading.com) exists — they do the bolding technique. So:
- **Option A:** Call it Bionic Reader anyway — different enough (Reader vs Reading, neurodivergent focus vs speed reading). Risky if they trademarked broadly.
- **Option B:** Pivot name. Alternatives: **ReadLike**, **SamePage**, **BrainRead**, **Nexus Read**, **Aligned**, **MyRead**, **ReadForm**, **NeuroRead**
- **Option C:** Keep Bionic Reader, be distinct enough that there's no confusion.
