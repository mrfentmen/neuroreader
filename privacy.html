Privacy

We collect no data. Nothing leaves your browser. No accounts. No tracking.

The short version

NeuroReader collects nothing. There is no account system, no sign-up, no profile, no analytics, no cookies. We built no way to collect your data, so there is nothing to sell, nothing to leak, and nothing to misuse. This is not a policy — it is a founding vow.

Nothing leaves your browser

Every transformation happens locally, on your device, using the JavaScript that ships with this page. The text you paste is never sent to a server. It never touches the internet. When you close the tab, your text is gone — we could not retrieve it even if we wanted to. No data ever crosses your screen except the text you put there.

No accounts, no tracking

  • No cookies are set by NeuroReader.
  • No analytics scripts, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting.
  • No logins. We do not know who you are, how often you visit, or what you read.
  • The app works fully offline — you can save it and use it with no connection at all.

What about ads?

NeuroReader may show a single banner advertisement at the very bottom of the page. Ads never appear over the text you are reading — that is a vow, not a preference. Right now there are no ads and no third-party scripts of any kind. If ads are ever added, they will come from a privacy-respecting network, and this page will be updated to say exactly what that means.

Your text is yours

The text you paste into NeuroReader belongs to you. We never see it, never store it, and never share it. Transformations happen in milliseconds, entirely on your device.

Changes to this policy

If anything here ever changes, this page will be updated first, in plain language, and the changelog will say so. NeuroReader is built in public — the whole project history is open for anyone to read.

Free. Always. Built for neurodivergent brains.
