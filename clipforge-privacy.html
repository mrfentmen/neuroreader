Privacy
Policy

ClipForge — Local Video Compressor is designed to process your selected media on your device.

Effective date: August 11, 2026

What ClipForge handles

When you choose a local video file, ClipForge reads that file in Chrome to preview it and create a smaller browser-native WebM copy. The file is processed locally in the extension page and is not uploaded to a developer server.

What is stored

ClipForge uses Chrome's local extension storage only to remember your last selected compression preset. It does not store video contents, filenames, video metadata, browsing history, account information, or analytics in developer-controlled storage.

What is not collected or shared

  • No video or image uploads to ClipForge servers.
  • No browsing history, webpage content, host access, or content scripts.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising network, or remote ad code.
  • No sale or sharing of user media or extension data.

Advertising and support links

The extension displays two static advertisement placeholders. They do not load advertising scripts or tracking code. The optional Buy Me a Coffee link opens an independent third-party website only when you click it; ClipForge does not attach your video or extension data to that link.

Permissions and security

ClipForge requests only the storage permission, for the saved preset described above. It has no host permissions, no content scripts, no remote executable code, and a strict extension-page content security policy.

No browser extension can be guaranteed “unhackable.” This policy describes the intended behavior of the code published in the ClipForge package.
