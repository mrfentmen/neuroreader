## Asset rules

- Store icons are exactly 128×128 PNG files.
- Screenshots are exactly 1280×800 or 640×400 PNG files.
- Upload the icon in the Store icon field and the screenshots in the Screenshots section.
- Do not upload README files or SVG source files as store screenshots.
- Do not show personal files, private filenames, account details, or unrelated platform branding.
- Do not use screenshots to imply MP4 output, website downloading, guaranteed security, or password recovery.
