ClipForge processes videos locally in Chrome. The extension does not upload, collect, sell, or share video files, filenames, video metadata, or usage analytics.

Data practices

ClipForge requests only the storage permission to remember the last compression preset. It has no host permissions, content scripts, remote code, server, account system, or advertising network.

When you click Buy Me a Coffee, Chrome opens that independent third-party website. ClipForge does not attach your video or any extension data to that link.

The two static advertisement placeholders are not connected to an ad network and do not load scripts or tracking code.

To remove the locally stored preset, remove the extension from Chrome. To contact the maintainer, use the support address shown in the extension.
